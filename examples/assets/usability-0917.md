# Usability check, 17 September

Five buyers from small fabrication shops tried the portal prototype.

- **Pricing is what they came for.** Four of five went straight to Pricing; two missed "See pricing" on Home because it sat below the photo.
- **Quotes need a lead time.** Every buyer asked how long a quote takes before they filled the form. Show "Quotes in one working day" next to the button.
- **The rack photo builds trust.** Three said it made the shop feel real. Keep it, but not above the prices.
- **Plan names confuse.** "Workshop" and "Foundry" read as industries, not sizes. Lead with the volume each plan covers.

Next: move "See pricing" above the fold, add the lead time to the quote form, rename plans by volume.
