# Branding

A brand should be felt everywhere and shouted nowhere.

## Where brand lives
- Accent colour, typeface choice, imagery style, illustration, tone of voice and a few signature details (a radius, a pattern, a motion).
- The logo appears once, usually top-left in the nav. It does not repeat in every section or card.

## Restraint
- Brand expression is strongest on marketing pages, onboarding and empty states, and quietest in dense working screens where people need to focus.
- Standard controls stay recognisable: a branded button is still clearly a button.
- Do not let brand colour replace semantic colour (a red brand still needs a distinct danger style).

## Direction
Before styling, set and write the direction (direction skill): who it is for, the surface mode, palette, type and layout with their reasons, the one bold thing, and what it must never feel like. Every choice traces back to it.

## Imagery
- One consistent style: photography or illustration, not a mix. Same lighting, crop and colour treatment throughout.
- Imagery shows the product or the outcome, not generic stock people pointing at screens.
- Reference images are for feel; write your own content and never copy another brand's marks.

## Voice
- Pick a voice (plain and friendly is right for most products) and keep it across headlines, buttons and errors. See ux-writing.
