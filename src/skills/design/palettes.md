# Starting points by kind of product

These calibrate a direction: they show what tends to suit a kind of product and why. They're starting points, not answers. Take the reasoning, then change at least one axis (a colour from the subject's own world, a different face, a layout move) so the result belongs to this brief. Check the result against the tells skill: a starting point copied unchanged is itself a default. Every face listed is one buni embeds, so it draws on the canvas, in screenshots and on the exported site (typography skill).

## Money, trading and operations dashboards
- Mode operate; variance 2, motion 2, density 8. People scan, compare and act fast, for hours.
- Palette: Ledger #F6F7F9 bg, Graphite #1E2430 ink, Slate #5B6575 ink-2, Signal #2457D6 action, Gain #1F8A5B, Loss #C2412D.
- Type: Schibsted Grotesk for interface, JetBrains Mono only for figures and codes, with tabular numbers.
- Layout: fixed sidebar, dense tables with right-aligned numbers, one key chart per view, filters in the URL.
- Avoid: near-black with acid green, hero metrics with sparklines on every card, red and green as the only signal.

## Health and care
- Mode operate or read; variance 2, motion 1, density 4. Trust, calm, legibility under stress, older and low-vision readers.
- Palette: Linen #FAF8F4 bg, Ink #1F2A33, Sage #4F6F63 action, Mist #E6EEEA tint, Alert #B3412E.
- Type: Figtree for everything (open shapes, clear at small sizes); optionally Source Serif 4 for long reading.
- Layout: one task per screen, large targets, plain steps with progress, generous line height.
- Avoid: clinical blue-and-white cliché, small grey text, playful motion around medical facts.

## Food, restaurants and hospitality
- Mode persuade (and operate for ordering); variance 6, motion 4, density 3. Appetite, place and warmth.
- Palette: taken from the menu itself, e.g. Saffron #E2A62F, Basil #3E6A3A, Plum #5A2948, Crust #F3E7D3, Char #2A2420.
- Type: Playfair Display for headlines, DM Sans for menus and details.
- Layout: big real photography of the food and the room, menu as readable lists (not cards), the booking or order action always reachable.
- Avoid: cream and terracotta by default, stock photos of people laughing, tiny menu text over images.

## Children and learning (8–12)
- Mode experience and operate; variance 7, motion 6, density 3. Delight, big targets, reading levels kept low.
- Palette: Sky #4DA3FF, Sun #FFC83D, Grass #3FBF6F, Berry #E2487A on Cloud #FFFFFF with Night #23233A ink.
- Type: Unbounded for headings, Figtree for text, both at generous sizes.
- Layout: one action per screen, chunky shapes, characters or illustration from the subject, progress you can see.
- Avoid: pastel everything (low contrast), tiny controls, dark patterns around rewards.

## Developer tools
- Mode operate and read; variance 3, motion 2, density 7. Precision, speed, keyboard first.
- Palette: Paper #FBFBFA bg (or Graphite #17181B for a dark tool used at night), Ink #1B1C1F, Steel #6B6F76, Indigo #4B57E3 action, Amber #C98A1B warning.
- Type: Instrument Sans or Space Grotesk for interface, JetBrains Mono for code only.
- Layout: command palette, shortcuts shown inline, docs with a narrow measure and a visible table of contents, copyable code blocks.
- Avoid: monospace for prose and labels, black with acid green, "terminal" costume on things that aren't a terminal.

## Luxury, fashion and beauty
- Mode persuade and experience; variance 7, motion 4, density 1. Space, restraint, material.
- Palette: Ivory #F7F3EE bg, Espresso #2B211C ink, Taupe #8C7B6E, Gilt #B08A4E accent used rarely.
- Type: EB Garamond or Playfair Display for display, Manrope for small text; large sizes, wide margins.
- Layout: full-bleed imagery, asymmetric compositions, very few words per screen, slow deliberate reveals.
- Avoid: busy grids, many accents, discount-style badges, glassmorphism.

## Public services and government
- Mode operate and read; variance 1, motion 1, density 4. Everyone must succeed, including on old phones and with assistive tech.
- Palette: White #FFFFFF bg, Ink #1D2733, Civic #1D5DA8 action, Focus #FFD23F, Error #C12A2A, adjusted to the country's own identity.
- Type: Source Serif 4 and Instrument Sans, or the country's own type system (then say the builder must supply it).
- Layout: one thing per page, questions one at a time, plain language at a low reading age, no decoration.
- Avoid: marketing heroes, clever copy, anything that only works on a big screen.

## Outdoors, travel and nature
- Mode persuade and experience; variance 6, motion 4, density 3. Place, scale, weather and light.
- Palette from the landscape, e.g. Fjord #2D4F5E, Lichen #A8B48A, Granite #6F7470, Dusk #E8B07A, Snow #F5F6F3.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque for display, DM Sans for text.
- Layout: photography or maps lead, routes and itineraries as real sequences (numbering is earned here), conditions and dates easy to find.
- Avoid: generic stock vistas, gradient skies as background washes.

## Studios and portfolios
- Mode experience; variance 8, motion 5, density 2. The work is the content; the interface recedes.
- Palette: chosen around the work, often near-neutral (Bone #EEEBE5, Soot #1A1A1A) so the work's colour leads.
- Type: one characterful family used with confidence, e.g. Syne for display with Instrument Sans for text.
- Layout: big work, unusual but navigable structure, case studies as readable stories.
- Avoid: the "big bold sans name plus scrolling marquee" default, cursor gimmicks that slow navigation.

## Business software (B2B, productivity)
- Mode operate; variance 3, motion 2, density 6. Daily use, many tables and forms, clarity over brand.
- Palette: Porcelain #F7F8FA bg, Ink #1C2230, Pewter #667085, a brand accent with one job (e.g. Teal #127C78), state colours.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans or Figtree with tabular figures.
- Layout: sidebar plus content plus inspector, tables over cards, empty states that teach the first task.
- Avoid: the SaaS card kit (identical rounded cards, same shadow, gradient washes), dashboards of meaningless metrics.

## Home, garden and hobbies
- Mode operate with a warm voice; variance 4, motion 2, density 3. A small daily ritual, glanced at briefly.
- Palette from the hobby's materials, e.g. Soil #3B3127, Leaf #4E7A3A, Clay pot #B46A4B (used sparingly), Paper #F5F2EA, Stem #7E8F6A.
- Type: a friendly serif for headings (Fraunces) with a plain sans for actions (Figtree).
- Layout: today's few things first, clear actions per item, history and settings tucked away.
- Avoid: the cream-serif-terracotta default unless the subject truly earns it; cute illustration that slows the daily task.
